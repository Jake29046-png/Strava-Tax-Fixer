Getting your activity file

To edit an activity, you first need the actual .FIT, .TCX, or .GPX file off of Strava or Garmin Connect. Here's exactly where to find it. All of this happens on their website (strava.com / connect.garmin.com) — the mobile apps don't offer file export.

Strava
From the activity page, on strava.com (desktop site, not the app)
GPXTCXOriginal (often FIT)
  1. Log in at strava.com and open the activity you want.
  2. On the activity page, find the "···" (more) menu — it's on the left side, below the activity title.
  3. For a GPX file, click "Export GPX". It downloads immediately.
  4. For the richest data (heart rate, cadence, power), you want either "Export Original" from that same menu — this gives you the raw file your device recorded, usually .FIT — or a TCX file: Strava doesn't put TCX in the menu, but you can get one by adding /export_tcx to the end of the activity's URL and hitting enter (e.g. strava.com/activities/12345678/export_tcx).

Which to pick: "Export Original" is almost always the best choice if it's there — it's the exact file your watch/device produced, before Strava touched it. GPX is the safest fallback (works for any GPS activity) but strips out estimated power. TCX sits in between — has heart rate, cadence, and power, just not everything a native FIT file has.

Garmin Connect
From the activity page, on connect.garmin.com
FITGPXTCX
  1. Log in at connect.garmin.com, click Activities in the left menu, then open the activity you want.
  2. On the activity page, find the gear/settings icon in the upper-right corner.
  3. Click it to open a dropdown with four options: Export Original (the raw .FIT file — every data point your watch recorded), Export to TCX, Export to GPX, and Export to Google Earth (KML — not usable here).
  4. Pick the one you want and it downloads straight away.

Which to pick: Export Original (.FIT) is the best choice on Garmin — it's the native format and has everything: power, cadence, heart rate, GPS, and more. Only use TCX or GPX if you specifically need those formats for something else.

Downloading everything at once

Strava: Account settings → "Download or Delete Your Account" → "Request Your Archive". Strava emails you a link to a ZIP of every activity's original file.

Garmin Connect: click your username (top right) → "Account Settings" → "Export Your Data". Garmin prepares a full export, typically ready within 24–48 hours.

Once you've got the file downloaded (it'll be in your phone's Files app, or your computer's Downloads folder), head back to Strava Tax Fixer and drop it in.
